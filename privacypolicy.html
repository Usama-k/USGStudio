Privacy Policy - USGStudio

Last updated: July 2026

USGStudio ("we," "us," or "our") develops and publishes mobile games. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you play our games. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By playing our games, you agree to the terms of this privacy policy.

1. We Do Not Collect Personal Information

We respect your privacy. Our games are designed to be played without requiring you to provide any personal data. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location) when you play our games.

2. No Third-Party Services or Analytics

Our games operate completely independently. We do not use any third-party analytics tools, tracking pixels, or data collection services. Your gameplay data and progress are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.

3. No Advertisements

Our games do not contain any third-party advertisements or advertising networks. Therefore, no Advertising IDs or tracking cookies are collected or used.

4. No In-App Purchases

Our games do not offer any in-app purchases or microtransactions. We do not process, collect, or have access to any of your financial or billing information.

5. Information Collected by App Stores

While we do not collect any data, please be aware that the app distribution platform you used to download our game (such as the Google Play Store or Apple App Store) may collect basic, anonymous analytics data (such as download counts, uninstall rates, and crash reports) according to their own privacy policies. We only have access to aggregated, non-identifiable data provided by these platforms to help us understand how our games are performing globally.

6. Policy for Children

Because our games do not collect any personal information, they are safe for users of all ages. However, if a specific game has an age restriction due to its content, players must adhere to the age ratings provided on the app store.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy frequently to be informed of how we are protecting your information.
