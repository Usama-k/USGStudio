Privacy Policy for Classic Snake Game

Effective Date: July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RIORASH LLC ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use the Classic Snake Game mobile application ("App").

About the App

Classic Snake Game is a simple offline arcade game developed by RIORASH LLC. The App is designed for entertainment and does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.

The App does not require:

  • User registration
  • Login or authentication
  • Access to contacts
  • Access to location
  • Access to camera
  • Access to microphone
  • Access to photos or files
  • Any personal information from users

Offline Functionality

Classic Snake Game is designed to work offline. The App does not communicate with our servers because we do not operate any backend services for this App.

Third-Party Services

This App does not use third-party services that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

Classic Snake Game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store user data, there is no personal information maintained by RIORASH LLC through this App.
